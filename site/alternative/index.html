THE IDEA IS SIMPLE

Keep an eye on the work,
without leaving your chat.

The Office reads the activity your coding tools already save on your machine and turns it into an animated office beside your Claude chat.

Use Live to see who is busy and who needs you. Look back through Today, Week, and Month to see where your agents spent their time. What they did groups their tool calls into coding, tests, reading, and more.

What does “agent-time” mean?

Combined active time across agent sessions. Several agents can work at once, so a day can add up to more than 24 agent-hours.

  • LIVEMoves while they work.

  • ONE OFFICEYour agents, under one roof.

  • LOCALNothing leaves your machine.
